<p><b>Quick Timer keeps your countdown within reach.</b> Start a timer from Quick Settings, then check, pause, resume or cancel it from the notification while using other apps.</p>
<p>Use it as a kitchen timer while cooking, time your tea or coffee, take a short break, or set a study session. Open the app for a large, clear countdown and add a minute when you need more time.</p>
<p><b>Simple everyday timing</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Start from the app or your Quick Settings tile.</li>
<li>Set hours, minutes and seconds with haptic feedback.</li>
<li>See the countdown and pause, resume or cancel from the notification.</li>
<li>Add one minute from the timer screen.</li>
<li>Choose from five built-in alarm sounds.</li>
<li>See a low alarm volume warning when setting a timer from the tile.</li>
<li>Use a light or dark theme that follows your phone.</li>
<li>No ads or account required.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Add the Timer tile</b></p>
<ol>
<li>Swipe down to open Quick Settings.</li>
<li>Tap Edit or the pencil icon.</li>
<li>Move the Timer tile into your active tiles.</li>
<li>Tap Timer, allow notifications when asked, choose a duration and start.</li>
</ol>
<p>Tile editing varies by phone. You can also open Quick Timer from your home screen to start and control a timer.</p>
<p>Quick Timer runs one countdown at a time.</p>
